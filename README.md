## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains **Capstone Project 2** for the Data Science & Machine Learning Program at **Purwadhika Digital Technology School**. 

The goal of this project is to analyze the sales performance and customer trends of a B2B Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) company. By conducting exploratory data analysis (EDA) and evaluating key business metrics, this project provides actionable insights to help optimize sales strategy, improve customer retention, and drive revenue growth.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem & Objectives
* **Identify High-Value Segments:** Determine which customer segments, regions, and industry verticals contribute most to overall revenue.
* **Analyze Sales Trends:** Evaluate monthly/quarterly sales performance to uncover seasonality and growth patterns.
* **Optimize Discount & Pricing Strategy:** Assess how discounts impact profit margins and unit economics.
* **Data-Driven Recommendations:** Formulate actionable, business-oriented strategies based on empirical data findings.

## 📊 Key Insights & Highlights

* **Revenue Drivers:** Top enterprise clients and specific key regions contribute the majority of recurring revenue.
* **Discount Impact:** Uncontrolled discounting significantly reduced overall profit margins without proportionally driving deal volume.
* **Product Performance:** Certain core SaaS product offerings consistently outperform legacy packages in cross-selling opportunities.
